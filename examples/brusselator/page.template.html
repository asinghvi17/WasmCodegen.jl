The Brusselator, a stiff reaction–diffusion system

Two chemical species diffusing along a line, solved by OrdinaryDiffEq's Rodas5P in your browser. Each step factorizes W = I/(γ·dt) − J and solves with it; which of LinearSolve's pure-Julia solvers does that is yours to pick. All of it is Julia compiled to WebAssembly, the integrators built at compile time and serialized into the module.
