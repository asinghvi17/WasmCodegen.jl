Julia's parser, running in your browser

The JuliaSyntax parser that ships with Julia 1.13 (Base.JuliaSyntax), compiled from Julia to WasmGC with WasmCodegen. Every edit is lexed and parsed in WebAssembly, error recovery included; the page reads back the parser's raw output, tokens and tree ranges, and builds the green tree from it as build_tree does.

      The whole parser
      
      
      JuliaSyntax's lexer and recursive-descent parser, unchanged: __FUNCS__ Julia functions in
        __SIZE__ KB of WebAssembly. Its tables (kind names, operator sets) were built when Julia loaded
        and are serialized into the module: __OBJECTS__ objects.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      What WebAssembly lacks
      
      
      The lexer asks libutf8proc about Unicode characters, and numbers go through C's
        strtod. Here the Unicode tables come from the native libraries at build time and
        floats are parsed by Parsers.jl, compiled into the module with the parser.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Checked against Julia
      
      
      On 308 KB of Julia source, Base files included, the module's output matches native
        JuliaSyntax node for node: kinds, spans, flags and diagnostics.
